Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

No data collected by the app

The Tempodrome app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no analytics, no accounts, no advertising, and no network connections beyond the local Bluetooth/WiFi communication between your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Waitlist email collection

If you choose to join the Tempodrome launch waitlist, we collect your email address solely to notify you when the app becomes available on the App Store. Your email will never be shared with third parties, used for advertising, or contacted for any purpose other than the Tempodrome launch. You can request removal from the waitlist at any time by emailing support@tempodrome.com. Waitlist data is stored securely via Tally.so — see their privacy policy at tally.so/privacy.

Apple Watch connectivity

Communication between iPhone and Apple Watch uses Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This data stays entirely on your devices and is never sent to any server.

No third-party SDKs

Tempodrome contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No data about your usage is shared with any third party.

Contact

If you have any questions about privacy, contact us at hello@tempodrome.com
